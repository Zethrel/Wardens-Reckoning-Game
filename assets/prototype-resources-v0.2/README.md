# Resource test pictures, refinements (TEST v0.2, 15-11-2026)

The refinements of batches 2 and 3, from the package
`WR_Refinements_Batches_02_03_TEST_v0.2` (kept with the implementation
planning). They redraw five kinds:
- stone, with stronger small piles;
- grain and flour, as loose piles without sacks;
- vegetables and fruit, with clearer shapes.

In the asset preview's resource board, L shows these in place of v0.1's.

They were made the same way as v0.1's (see its README), at 32 pixels a tile
(`compact/`) and 64 (`fine/`). The package's names were right, and grain and
flour drop their `_loose` here.

The package's own colour cleanup darkened the red apples, as its README
warned; they look dark red to brown here.
