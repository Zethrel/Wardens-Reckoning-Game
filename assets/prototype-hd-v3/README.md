# High-detail vegetation experiment (v3, 14-11-2026)

The v2 trees with the 32-colour palette rebalanced (24 leaf greens and 8 bark
browns), so the trunks are brown again; the same silhouettes, sizes and feet as
v2. An experiment to compare in the asset preview (`src/UI/AssetPreview.tscn`:
T goes past the sets to it, H switches between compact and full size, W fills a
test wood). It does not replace v0.8, and the game does not use it.

- `tree_mature_deciduous_hd_v3_compact`: 64 × 96, its foot at (32, 95).
- `tree_mature_conifer_hd_v3_compact`: 32 × 96, its foot at (16, 95).
- `tree_mature_deciduous_hd_v3_full`: 128 × 192, its foot at (64, 191).
- `tree_mature_conifer_hd_v3_full`: 64 × 192, its foot at (32, 191).

The whole package (with its palette) and its review are kept with the
implementation planning (`03 - Asset Prototypes`).
