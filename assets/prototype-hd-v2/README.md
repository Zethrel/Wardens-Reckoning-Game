# High-detail vegetation experiment (v2, 14-11-2026)

The high-detail v1 trees with clear-or-solid edges, their roots on the bottom
row and a shared 32-colour palette, each in two sizes. An experiment to compare
in the asset preview (`src/UI/AssetPreview.tscn`: T goes past the sets to it, H
switches between compact and full size, W fills a test wood). It does not
replace v0.8, and the game does not use it.

- `tree_mature_deciduous_hd_v2_compact`: 64 × 96, its foot at (32, 95).
- `tree_mature_conifer_hd_v2_compact`: 32 × 96, its foot at (16, 95).
- `tree_mature_deciduous_hd_v2_full`: 128 × 192, its foot at (64, 191).
- `tree_mature_conifer_hd_v2_full`: 64 × 192, its foot at (32, 191).

The whole package (with its palette) and its review are kept with the
implementation planning (`03 - Asset Prototypes`).
