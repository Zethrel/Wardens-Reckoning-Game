# High-detail vegetation experiment (v1, 14-11-2026)

Two trees of another kind than the approved v0.8 set: detailed, many-coloured
art at twice v0.8's size. An experiment to compare in the asset preview
(`src/UI/AssetPreview.tscn`: T goes past the sets to it, H halves its size). It
does not replace v0.8, and the game does not use it.

- `tree_mature_deciduous_hd_01`: 128 × 192, its foot at (64, 191).
- `tree_mature_conifer_hd_01`: 64 × 192, its foot at (32, 191).

The whole package and its review are kept with the implementation planning
(`03 - Asset Prototypes`).
